# Licensing and attribution

The source dataset releases **HaPy-Bug v1** and **CVEfixes v1.0.8** publish their
dataset material under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

This repository contains a reconstructed, filtered, combined and split research
derivative. Selection and assistant review judgments are additions made during
preparation; they are not endorsements or new annotations by the original
dataset authors. Source release identifiers and citations are retained in
[`attribution/`](attribution/).

**The original licences, copyright notices and terms of individual upstream
software projects continue to apply to their source-code snippets and source
snapshots.** Dataset-level CC BY 4.0 terms do not replace those software terms.
Repository identities, paths, commit hashes and source URLs are preserved in
the records; complete archived modules retain their original notices where
present. An exhaustive per-file licence audit has not been completed.

The original CVEfixes notice is retained at
[`attribution/CVEFIXES_UPSTREAM_LICENSE.txt`](attribution/CVEFIXES_UPSTREAM_LICENSE.txt).
HaPy-Bug's official release metadata, including its licence and author list, is
retained at
[`attribution/HAPY_BUG_OFFICIAL_RELEASE_METADATA.json`](attribution/HAPY_BUG_OFFICIAL_RELEASE_METADATA.json).
